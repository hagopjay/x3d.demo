Educational proof of concept. Not medical advice, not a medical device, not for diagnosis. The v6 CT with one human-made organ label (pancreas, segment 5, FMA:7198). The other four colours are still HU bands, not organs. One patient, one annotator.
SegmentedVolumeData, X_ITE 16.6.0; intensity volume is the hash-checked v6 file. Drag to rotate. The MetadataSet on the Transform carries series UID, label file, zip hash, axis fix and the segment-to-FMA term.
