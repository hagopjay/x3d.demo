Medical provenance atlas FMA, SNOMED CT ids and H-Anim names on a CT torso, with a grade on every claim (v3: claims hashed into the scene)

Every claim carries a grade: C Confirmed (checked in this build, instrument named) L Likely (third-party or earlier build, not re-checked) S Suspected (judgement, scope doubt or gap).
Educational proof of concept. Not medical advice, not a medical device, no clinical use. The torso is one public CT subject; the brain is a template and its fit to the torso is illustrative. A grade C means "this build checked it", not "an anatomist approved it".
