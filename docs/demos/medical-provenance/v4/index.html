Jointed H-Anim skeleton 35 joints and 35 segments built from CT torso bones, with FMA classes on the segments (v4)

Grades: C checked in this build L likely, not re-checked S estimate, judgement or gap.
Educational proof of concept. Not medical advice, not a medical device. Joint centres are estimated from bone meshes of one public CT subject; the skeleton has no joint limits and is not a biomechanical model.
Pick a joint, or choose a pose. Blue translucent shapes are ribs, sternum and cartilage: H-Anim has no segment for them.
