Where the model is right, and where it confidently isn't

v1 had no ground truth. v2 runs the same Open3D-ML RandLA-Net (trained on finished offices) on three real construction shells from Rohbau3D (CC BY 4.0), maps the dataset's 18 classes to the model's 13 only where the meaning matches, and scores every point. The X3D scene carries two extra lenses: correct (green right, red wrong, grey not scored) and ground truth.
