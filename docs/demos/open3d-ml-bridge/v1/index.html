A neural segmentation, held accountable in X3D

Open3D-ML's RandLA-Net runs on a plain CPU over a real RGB-D room scan. Its output stops being a coloured blob: every class becomes an X3D group carrying its id, name, count, share and mean confidence as MetadataSet, three switchable lenses (class, source colour, confidence) share one Coordinate, and the whole file reads straight back into Open3D. The model, checkpoint hash, library versions and validator verdict ride in the file's own <head>.
