How Summit Duel works

v1 to v3 replay one fall that Blender's Bullet solver computed in advance. v4 simulates every duel live in your browser, so each one is new. It reads v3's own files: the 203 course heights come from v1's seeded generator, the H-Anim joint centres from v3's fall.x3d, and the pyramid you see is v3's baked geometry and textures, read from that X3D by a small loader on this page.

What is simulated

PartHow
Bodies and contactsRapier 0.21, deterministic build, 120 steps per second, 8 solver iterations. 202 courses are static boxes. The capstone course is a kinematic slab of 12 × 12 tiles, which can tilt and be painted.
Duellists11 rigid bodies each, placed at v3's H-Anim joint centres, with v1's masses (80 kg), friction 0.45 and restitution 0.15. Hand-written joint motors (PD torques on quaternion error) hold a pose. "Puppet strings", an upright torque and a support force under the pelvis, keep the figure on its feet while its balance lasts. Hits lower balance; at 0.12 the figure goes limp, and it gets up again unless it is below the summit.
Paintball pistolsPellets leave the real forearm, so a wobbling arm shoots wide. They fly at 45 m/s under gravity, and in a Magnetic Storm under the Lorentz force q v × B, with a curve radius of v / (6 B). A hit pushes with 13 N·s. Pink paint adds 35 % to every later push. On the floor, blue paint lowers a tile's friction to 0.035, yellow raises it to 2.2, and green makes it bouncy.
OmensLightning, a magnetic storm, the Scales of Anubis (g × 0.35 and × 1.75), the Eye of Ra, the Khamsin, a time eddy (velocity × ¼ inside, gravity × 1/16), the Duat gates, the Benben tilt, the Ascension (gravity points up for 1.8 s), the Khafre battery, the orbital slug and a paint monsoon. Their strengths are game constants, not measurements.
ShellsDrag-free arcs from Khafre's summit, solved for the flight time when they are fired, so the shadow marker shows exactly where each one will land. Each blast is an impulse that falls off with the square of distance.
The fallA figure counts as fallen when its pelvis is 1.6 m below the summit. From then on it is a limp ragdoll on the stepped face, and hits are counted the way v1 counted them: m |Δv − g Δt| per body, above 18 N·s, at most once per body every 40 ms.

Rewind and the Ankh

Every step is recorded, so the timeline can be reviewed forwards, backwards and slowly. That review shows the recorded poses; it does not run the simulation. Every 2 s the page also keeps a complete checkpoint: Rapier's snapshot, every controller, the omen state and the random number generator. ☥ Ankh restores the checkpoint before the moment on screen, re-simulates to that exact step with your clicks, and then reseeds fate, so the past is the same and the future is new.

What I checked, and what I did not

See README.md and receipts/ in this folder. Briefly: the same seed and the same clicks give the same per-step hash in Node and in headless Chromium, and a restored checkpoint continues to the identical hash. Controls that should fail do fail: restoring a checkpoint without the controllers or without the random numbers gives a different hash. Every simulation file is free of Math.sin, cos, atan2, exp and pow. I did not measure on a real GPU or a phone, and I did not test Firefox or Safari. No person has played this yet.
