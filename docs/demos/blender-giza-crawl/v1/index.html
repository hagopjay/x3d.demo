Into the pyramid. Down the shaft. Into the legend.

An explorer built as an H-Anim 2.0 humanoid walks, stoops, crawls and climbs a 7.5 minute route: in at the Great Pyramid's north entrance, up through the Grand Gallery to the King's Chamber, down the well shaft to the Subterranean Chamber cut into bedrock, then 30 m down the three levels of the Osiris Shaft, and finally into a Hall of Records that nobody has ever found. How the explorer moves

Seconds in each gait

One 16-joint H-Anim 2.0 figure with a headlamp. Six gaits share one parameter set (torso tilt, pelvis height, hip, knee and arm angles, swing amplitudes) and blend into each other over 0.7 s when the ceiling changes. The passage height chooses the gait: walk above 1.7 m, stoop in the 1.2 m passages, crawl in the 0.9 m passage to the Subterranean Chamber, climb in the shafts.
