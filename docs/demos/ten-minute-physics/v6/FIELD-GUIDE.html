1The order of a method depends on where you measure it.
Symplectic Euler shows order 1.00 measured at T = 1 and 2.00 at one full period T = 2 pi; explicit Euler reads 1.10 there. The leading error term can cancel over a full period. Fit at a time that is not a multiple of the period.		harmonic oscillator, 5 step sizes eachConfirmed
2Bounded energy is not accuracy.
On a Kepler orbit (e = 0.6, 200 steps per orbit) velocity Verlet keeps the energy error at 0.74% for 50 orbits with no growth (ratio 1.0000001) and is 0.61 (orbit radius units) off the true position after 5 orbits. RK4 drifts in energy (6.93e-05 to 3.35e-04) but is 0.0022 off: 283 times better. The phase error is the one that a picture shows.		Kepler, exact position from Newton on Kepler's equationConfirmed
3A symplectic method's advantage is asymptotic, and I did not measure the crossover.
At 200 steps per orbit RK4 beat Verlet on both energy (3.35e-04 against 7.35e-03) and position over 50 orbits. RK4's energy error was still rising when I stopped, so a crossing very likely exists later; I did not run to it.		Kepler, 50 orbitsSuspected (the crossing)
4Sweep along the long axis.
200 boxes in a 4 x 1 field: sweeping x needs 1002 box tests per frame, sweeping y 4133 (4.1 times more), brute force 19900. Pick the axis with the largest spread.		mean of 180 framesConfirmed
5Sweep and prune is not O(n) at constant density.
Tests per frame: 355, 1010, 2878, 8076 for 100, 200, 400, 800 boxes at fixed density and aspect: exponent 1.50. The reason is that each box tests every box whose x range it overlaps, and that count grows with the field height.		counted tests, 20 frames eachConfirmed (the exponent); Likely (the reason)
6Sort by the left edge, not by the centre.
Sorting by centre with the usual early exit missed 905 overlapping pairs over 180 frames. A large box's left edge is far from its centre.		same frames, brute-force pair setsConfirmed
7Morton codes: test the bit trick against the slow loop, including the corners.
100,008 inputs (random, all four corners, the alternating patterns 0xAAAA / 0x5555), 0 mismatches.		naive bit-by-bit interleaveConfirmed
8A Morton-sorted tree gives log-like queries from one sort.
4096 boxes, 1000 random queries: 78.6 tests per query against 4096 (1.92%), 0 queries different from brute force, about 5.2 hits each. 12 levels deep (log2 n = 12), so about 15 tests per hit.		set equality with an O(n) searchConfirmed
9Node boxes must enclose their children, exactly.
Shrinking every node box by 0.02 lost 2466 hits over 1000 queries. A tree that is a little too tight is silently wrong, not slow.		same queriesConfirmed
10A mutation control with a zero limit cannot fail.
My first 'limit x 10' edits on references whose limit is 0 (pairs missed, mismatches) changed nothing and the checker accepted them: two of eleven controls were vacuous. Pick a reference with a nonzero limit, and count the controls that were caught.		found by running them, fixed in witness/mutate.pyConfirmed
