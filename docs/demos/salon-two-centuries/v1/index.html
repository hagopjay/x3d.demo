Salon of Two Centuries: art from 1700 to 1899

A walkable X3D museum where one metre is one year. The spine corridor runs 200 m from 1700 to 1899. Each of the nine movements on the owner's art history chart is a gallery beside it, built in its own style and running from its start year to its end year, so overlapping movements sit side by side. Public-domain paintings hang at their real size on the wall, at their own year. Three of them open into rooms you can walk into. New pigments stand in the corridor in the year they arrived, and above it all the Atlas sky traces where 400 painters lived and worked, with arcs for who influenced and who taught whom.
