Gobbler, bird first

This page boots with the bird alone: the Gobbler 3 Deep head and knowledge graph, the Sun in its chest, the Dyson ribcage of 364 ontology classes, feathers modelled in Blender with golden-hour light baked in, and the tail fan. The Sun is the validator: it beats a replay of 30 real validator runs, a gold beat for a clean or repaired scene and a red one for every failing round. The tail fan's export paths are simulated for now and tagged so. The world loads only as you fly toward it: the Earth and the Moon sit inside the bird, and on the Earth stands a village (a plaza with a fountain and twelve villagers, modelled in Blender, exported to X3D, the light baked in). Open ?ui=none for a clean frame, ?seed=N repeats a take of the camera.

This public copy is drawn by Babylon.js 9.32.0 in WebGPU mode through our own X3D reader. In its knowledge graph, entries that touched private notes, people, costs or accounts are shown as “(redacted)” or “A person” and carry no text; the structure, counts and positions are unchanged.
