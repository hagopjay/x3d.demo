Spring Bone Lab · Demo 87

Four rigs, one X3D file, two PROTOs. Every wobble below is a SpringBoneChain written in plain X3D 4.0 and run by its own Script in X_ITE. Drag the figure, the fox or the glass ball. Tune in hertz and damping ratio; the solver turns them into exact per-step coefficients.

Switch the model to POSITION (the Unity asset's rule) and ask for ζ below 0.1: the "behaves like" line shows the floor that model cannot go under. FORCE reaches any ζ.

Where this comes from

Clean-room spring bones: the behaviour spec, solver and witnesses are in x3d.loom/x3d.springbone (14 witness rows against closed-form damped-spring answers, each with a planted control that must fail). The same solver ships for Blender (x3d-blender-io, bake to keys) and Unity (x3d.unity_io). Rigs, figure and geometry here are our own, CC0. Renderer: X_ITE 16.8.1 (MIT, vendored with its licence).
