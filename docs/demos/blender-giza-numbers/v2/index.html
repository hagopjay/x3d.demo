09 / SENSITIVITY LABEXPLICIT ASSUMPTIONS

Uncertainty is a model you must specify.

Try a hypothetical joint Gaussian model for base and independently estimated height. Correlation matters. These controls are an experiment, not a reanalysis of the archaeological surveys.

2,400 deterministic samples · first-order propagation

If height is derived as h = (b/2) tan θ, then 2b/h = 4/tan θ: base cancels. Do not assign b and h independent survey errors in that model.

10 / CONSTRUCTION SCALEIDEAL SOLID MODEL

Most of the work is near the ground.

An ideal pyramid holds 87.5% of its volume below half-height. Its centre of mass is h/4. Change assumed density and block mass to explore scale, rather than infer an actual construction schedule.

ENVELOPE MASS 6.75 MtBLOCK EQUIVALENTS 2.70 MIDEAL LIFT ENERGY 2.43 TJ

M = ρV; E = Mg(h/4), using g = 9.80665 m/s². No voids, bedrock core, material variation, quarrying, transport, friction or human efficiency. This is gravitational potential energy, not total construction energy.

11 / UNITS & INVARIANTSSAME GEOMETRY

Some numbers survive a change of units.

Lengths change their numerical values when the unit changes. Dimensionless ratios survive. That distinction separates geometric relationships from coincidences chosen in feet, miles or modern SI digits.
