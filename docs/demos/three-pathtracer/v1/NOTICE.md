# Path-traced X3D: sources

Libraries (MIT, vendored in `vendor/`): three r187 (`three.module.js`, `three.core.js`, `Pass.js` from examples/jsm), three-mesh-bvh 0.9.18, three-gpu-pathtracer 0.0.28. `vendor/bundle-body.js` is built by `x3d.loom/x3d.threejs/bridge/tools/bundle.js`. `src/pt-adapter.js` is `x3d.loom/x3d.threejs/pathtracer/src/pt-adapter.js`. `scenes/` are the x3d.lab Leg E scenes (generated by HagopJay and Claude, 15 Nov 2026). Table and strips: `x3d.threejs/pathtracer/build_results.py` (`index.tpl.html` is the template; `index.html` is its output).
