Masonic Lodge XR

A cutaway of the Great Pyramid, drawn from X3D, standing over each Masonic building OpenStreetMap lists near you. Public data only: building name and position. No member data, no meeting times, no rituals.

Idle. AR needs HTTPS, camera and location permission. GPS drift is typically 5 to 20 m outdoors, so the overlay is anchored to the lodge coordinate, not to the facade.

Sources and terms

  • Lodges: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, via Overpass API (read-only, cached 24 h per ~1 km cell, one query per cell). No national list is stored or published here; lodges load live for your position only.
  • Nearby articles: Wikipedia GeoSearch API (free, CORS). Giza dimensions: Wikipedia (Great Pyramid 146.6 m, base 230.34 m). Interior positions are schematic.
  • Sun azimuth and altitude: local low-precision solar formula (no network).
  • COGSNA and LodgeLocator are not used; their terms are unchecked.
