Segment Sphere: the wheel, wrapped and lifted to S³

The AWS Segment Wheel lays 301 services on a flat disc. Here the same 301 dots, with the same categories, rings, angles and lifecycle flags, become a ball you can turn, and then a 3-sphere: through the Hopf fibration every service becomes a great circle in four dimensions, projected into 3D. Any two of those circles link exactly once, so the catalogue turns into one interlocked chain mail of 45,150 links. One X3D Script morphs all three shapes; nothing is drawn by the page.

m = 0 · Wheel

Faithful to the source SVG: 3 rings, exact angles, wedge widths proportional to counts. Preview floats up 0.6, closed sinks 0.4, sunset sinks 2.6 below the plane.

m = 1 · Segment sphere

A lune's area is 2R²·Δφ, so it is proportional to its wedge angle and to its service count, exactly as on the wheel. The 29 sunset services fall to a red core, with a line from their old slot.

m = 2 · Hopf 3-sphere

Every point of the sphere has a circle above it in S³ (its fiber). Two fibers always link once: 301 services make C(301,2) = 45,150 linked pairs. An isoclinic 4D rotation tumbles the whole fibration while gems flow along their circles.
