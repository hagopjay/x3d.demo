# Giza V · Earth's Tesla Coil

## Intent

Build an instrument the visitor can enter: a schematic Great Pyramid becomes a giant resonant object; its layered materials, imagined conductive paths, water, and quartz can be examined; the view then moves into a king's chamber where an ankh and a Was-sceptre become the focus of a luminous journey. Wonder should come from both the experiment and the imagined experience.

v5 extends the v4 chamber prototype with a **bounded planetary-circuit proof of concept**. It keeps a strict distinction between an archaeological statement, a mathematical analogy, an unverified engineering hypothesis, and story invention. The same geometry can participate in several lenses, but its evidence status must remain visible and understandable. A dramatic rendering cannot upgrade a hypothesis into a finding.

A luminous canopy crowns the plateau; standing-looking arcs bridge the compressed sky; a reservoir answers a regional demand; and the circuit's rhythm reaches the king's ankh and Was-sceptre. This setting slowly depletes its stored energy; apparent visual continuity must not imply self-sustaining generation. Its default sky height is 80 km; the fictional two-mile canopy remains selectable.

Circuit **Run/Hold**, **Reset**, and **Precharge** are separate from journey playback. Holding a numerical circuit freezes its simulated energy time; pausing the 108-second story does not silently refill storage. Precharge is an explicit initialization from an unspecified external source, never energy extracted from resonance.

The phrase “dielectricity” can remain in the creative brief, but the source card should explain it briefly as a material's dielectric response, not a fluid or independently harvestable substance. Keep the product's normal controls concrete: altitude, capacitance, voltage, source power, demanded power, duty, loss, and stored energy.

## Creative direction

The visual identity combines near-black basalt tones, warm limestone, dark red granite, electrum-like gold, oxidized copper, deep lapis blue, and a narrow cyan-white excitation palette. v5 adds an atmospheric crown, tall controlled arcs, a glowing globe, and a Nile-region circuit map; keep their silhouettes distinct from the chamber's intimate focal objects. Use strong silhouettes, clearly separated depth layers, and an inhabited central chamber. Ornament should reveal a story or a controllable parameter rather than simply fill the screen.

The pyramid acts as an instrument in the story: inundation is its breath, stone is its tension, chambers are its intervals, and the ankh is its key. These metaphors are not physical derivations. A king's figure gives the scale and emotional centre. A visible ankh near the heart and a Was-sceptre near the head connect the intimate scene to the monumental machine. The Was's symbolic association with dominion informs the story; its luminous coupling is an invention. The visitor can accompany the king or enter a first-person perspective.

The story now deliberately reveals all three scales of encounter: celestial geometry, ancient machines and craft, and a nonhuman presence. These are escalating authored images rather than escalating evidence. Keep the Egyptian human and cultural context intact in every interpretation.

## Planetary mathematical and unit contracts

### Altitude and drawing scale

Use **1 international mile = 1.609344 km**, so the two-mile canopy is exactly **3.218688 km**. The UI's altitude range is 3.218688–300 km. The geometry maps this large physical range into a deliberately compressed scene height. `teslaResponse` uses the authored mapping \(h_{display}=320+180\log_{10}(h_{km}/3.218688)\). This is a display coordinate, not a physical height in the architectural scene's metre convention. Its label reports the entered physical altitude; a persistent cue identifies vertical compression. Neither a short apparent arc nor a sky mesh a few hundred scene units above the apex is a true-scale distance measurement.

The atmosphere card anchors the comparison to NASA's roughly 48 km lower extent and the region's variability. The 80 km reference is an authored representative sheet inside that broad region. Do not call an arbitrary two-mile sheet “the ionosphere.” Do not derive breakdown voltage, propagation time, or energy-capture rate from the compressed visual geometry.

### Reservoir capacity and live voltage

Convert entered capacitance and voltage to SI before doing any energy calculation:

\[
C_{\mathrm F}=C_{\mathrm{µF}}10^{-6},\qquad
V_{\mathrm V}=V_{\mathrm{MV}}10^6,\qquad
E_{\max}=\tfrac12 C V_{\mathrm{rated}}^2.
\]

\[
V_{\mathrm{live}}=\sqrt{2E/C},\qquad E_{\mathrm{GJ}}=E_{\mathrm J}/10^9.
\]

Stored energy is in **joules** or **gigajoules**; power is in **watts**, **megawatts**, or **gigawatts**; capacitance is in **farads** or **microfarads**. “Gigawatts stored as capacitance” becomes the concrete combination of reservoir capacitance, voltage, stored energy, and requested output power. One gigawatt delivered for ten seconds consumes ten gigajoules before losses; this is unit conversion, not a claim that Giza supplies it.

`createReservoir(settings, initialFraction = 0)` must make initial charge explicit; a zero argument/default starts empty, while the mythic preset deliberately passes a full initial fraction. The state and ledger must retain that initial energy. Reject invalid or nonfinite values and invalid fractions. Avoid division by zero when deriving voltage from capacitance.

### Supply ledger

For simulated elapsed time, track supplied energy, delivered arc energy, dissipated energy, remaining storage, and any energy rejected because the rated reservoir is full. The core accounting target is

\[
E_0+E_{\mathrm{source}}
=E_{\mathrm{stored}}+E_{\mathrm{arcs}}+E_{\mathrm{loss}}+E_{\mathrm{spill}},
\]

within a documented floating-point tolerance. A demand shortfall is reported as unmet demand, not credited as delivered energy. State energy stays in \(0\le E\le E_{\max}\). Source, loss, and output powers cannot silently add or subtract inconsistent MW/GW units.

For a requested arc output \(P_{\mathrm{arc}}\) and duty \(d\), the average demanded output is \(dP_{\mathrm{arc}}\). The source and loss controls are in MW and the arc display control in GW; the model stores arc rating as `arcMW`, so the UI adapter converts GW to MW before the SI model converts MW to watts. In the bounded teaching model the duty control is a time-averaged load, not a literal simulation of arc ignition/extinction. Surface commanded versus delivered output when energy becomes limiting. A depleted reservoir cannot keep delivering a nonzero demand without input or a disclosed model error. A full reservoir must not grow without limit.

`stepReservoir` advances constant rated powers through empty/full boundaries. Leakage has priority at an empty reservoir, and arcs receive only remaining supply. Disabling the bench disconnects its source and requested arcs while passive leakage remains. Lowering the rated capacity explicitly spills excess stored energy. At zero rated voltage the zero-energy bus supplies neither arcs nor leakage, and input spills. `unservedJ` records requested arc energy not delivered; it is not an additional energy sink in the conservation equation. These are disclosed teaching-model choices, not an electrical-protection design.

Precharge, reset, and rating changes must maintain an intelligible ledger. Reinitializing to a new full reservoir creates a **new explicitly initialized run**; it is not a charging observation. Session import restores assumptions, not unexplained previously stored gigajoules. Reset/reinitialization behavior belongs in the actual implementation receipt.

### LC analogy and separation from inherited models

The **reservoir** `capacitanceUF` and the LC **secondary** `secondaryPF` are separate elements. For the secondary's lumped ideal inductance and capacitance,

\[
f_{\mathrm{LC}}=\frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}.
\]

Convert `secondaryPF` by \(10^{-12}\) to farads and `inductanceMH` by \(10^{-3}\) to henries; `frequencyKHz` is the entered drive frequency. This is a component-model frequency, not a measured pyramid eigenmode or the frequency of the global atmospheric circuit. The response uses the assumed \(Q_{effective}=Q/(1+k/2)\), with coupling \(k\), and gain \(kM(r,Q_{effective})\) when enabled. This is an SDOF analogy, not a solved pair of coupled coils. Its bounded visual gain is \(g/(1+g)\), and its numerical gain is never multiplied into site voltage or source power. An LC model alone is not a full Tesla coil: real coils involve coupled circuits, geometry, losses, and input energy. No resonance gain is credited as newly created reservoir energy.

The module's pure public functions are `sanitizeTesla`, `teslaResponse`, `createReservoir`, `stepReservoir`, and `teslaChoreography`. The choreography derives bounded scene envelopes from the 108-second story. Arc brightness/availability must also reflect the reservoir's delivered demand rather than granting the story a new energy source. The controller maps delivered arc power to a bounded square-root brightness relative to 1 GW, with a story envelope; this does not alter the ledger. LC gain modulates the corona separately, so detuning does not silently change an independently rated arc load.

## Inherited chamber mathematical contracts

### Relative driven response

For a teaching oscillator with drive frequency ratio \(r=f/f_0\) and quality factor \(Q>0\), the conventional displacement magnification is

\[
M(r,Q)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1-r^2)^2+(r/Q)^2}}.
\]

An implementation may normalize this further for bounded display, but it must document that normalization. At \(r=1\), this expression is \(Q\); the precise peak can shift with damping. Avoid claiming that the damped peak is always exactly at \(f_0\). A source amplitude of zero must yield zero driven output, including at a resonance. The display may clamp graphical brightness without silently altering the underlying numerical result.

The prototype may use a reference frequency within the 0.50–1.50 MHz interval associated with the published paper's wavelength range. That reference is a scenario value, not a calculated eigenfrequency of this particular mesh. A curve from this equation does not establish a pyramid mode or validate the 2018 results. Real RF predictions require Maxwell's equations, geometry, material properties, boundary conditions, source specification, and solver convergence.

### Dimensionless charge/leakage analogy

The physical circuit analogy motivating the display is

\[
C\frac{dV}{dt}+\frac{V}{R}=d_{\mathrm{eff}}\frac{dF}{dt}.
\]

The first prototype uses normalized force, time, charge, and leakage variables. It should expose a bounded static-load step and/or a periodic change-of-force demonstration. Once the force stops changing, the source term becomes zero; a finite leakage path causes an exponential decay. An ideal zero-leakage capacitor can retain charge, but delivering energy to a connected load changes that condition. Sustained extracted power cannot be inferred from stone mass.

Do not label normalized voltage as volts or normalized power as watts. Crystal fraction and alignment are scenario assumptions, not a site measurement. Random orientations need not sum coherently. A macro-scale quartz-rich rock cannot be treated as one perfectly aligned piezoelectric crystal. No direct conversion from seasonal flooding to MHz drive is established.

The proposed 100,000 stones at two **metric tonnes** each give a 200,000,000 kg scenario. If a different ton convention is selected, the conversion changes. That scenario is separate from the actual pyramid's total mass. Contact area, stress tensor, load-bearing paths, elastic compliance, quartz fabric, electrodes, capacitance, leakage, and continuing mechanical work remain necessary for a quantitative model.

## The 108-second voyage

Six equal 18-second phases create a first directable story. Names below are creative targets; UI names may vary if timing and meaning remain clear. The timeline must derive its current phase and visual state from absolute journey time, so seeking and replaying do not depend on the path taken to reach a moment.

| 18–36 s | Compression | Stone, invented quartz, and imagined pathways become readable | Mechanical pressure is a hypothesis cue, not a source of endless current |

**Playback contract:** Play resumes from the current time; Pause stops time advancement; Seek sets a clamped time from 0 to 108 seconds; Restart returns to the beginning with a clear play state; completion remains stable at 108 seconds until explicitly replayed. An implementation that loops should label the loop and make it optional. Lens changes must not accidentally create competing animation loops. Audio, when present, starts only after an appropriate user gesture and can be stopped independently.

**Camera contract:** `cameraMode` is `witness` or `first-person`. Both sample choreography from the current absolute journey time. Switching midjourney or while paused must preserve that time, current phase, selected encounter cap, and lab assumptions. Witness mode offers an external view of the king and environment; first-person mode follows the authored embodied route with the king's body hidden. Avoid duplicate animation loops or a delayed named-view transition overwriting a manually switched journey camera. `journey.mjs` supplies the pure camera/reveal calculations; `app.mjs` applies them to X3D state.

**Reduced-motion contract:** Automatic camera travel freezes while the setting is active; the timeline, phase captions, seek controls, encounter cap, and evidence remain functional. The setting does not erase progress or force the user to leave the story. Check camera pose stability over multiple advancing frames; a paused visual pose must not be confused with a paused timeline.

Avoid global flashing. Favor smooth envelopes, material fades, and spatial movement over strobing. The most intense passage should retain a legible focal point rather than saturate the entire frame.

## Planetary experiments

1. **Spend the precharge.** Enter the mythic preset, inspect its 12.5 GJ initial energy, then remove the source while retaining a nonzero arc demand. Demand may remain selected; delivered energy must stop when the reservoir empties.
2. **Compare power with energy.** Keep the same stored energy and double the output demand. Explain the changed discharge duration from joules divided by watts, not from brighter geometry.
3. **Hold the ledger.** Freeze the circuit while the story continues. Resume and verify that energy accounting advances only with circuit time.
4. **Compare the sky.** Switch from the two-mile storm canopy to the 80 km reference and inspect the compression label. The visual change is not a newly validated electrical path.
5. **Try reference mode.** Contrast the 250 kV global-potential context and assumed local bench supply with the explicitly fictional mythic MW/GW supply. Notice which values are documented and which are selected assumptions.
6. **Inspect the network.** Use the regional map as a view of the authored system's reach. A lit beacon is a story output, not a measured historical load or a solved transmission line.

## Inherited chamber experiments

1. **Remove the source.** Tune the oscillator, then set the external drive to zero. Distinguish an object's capacity to resonate from a source that supplies energy.
2. **Add loss.** Compare low and high damping without changing the source. Observe the response curve instead of relying on brightness alone.
3. **Load and wait.** Apply the normalized static-load step. Watch a charge response decay through leakage. Contrast that with periodic changing force.
4. **Remove the invented bed.** Reduce coupling or quartz contribution to zero. This tests a model assumption; it does not conduct an archaeological survey.
5. **Compare interpretations.** Revisit the same scene in Archaeology, Laboratory, and Mythic. Identify which statements concern an object, an equation, and a story.
6. **Direct the journey.** Seek to the heart-gate moment, inspect it, return to the beginning, and replay. The narrative remains available for creative direction without disguising its invented status.
