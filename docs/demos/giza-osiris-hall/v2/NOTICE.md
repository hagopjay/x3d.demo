# Giza v2: the Osiris Shaft, the Hall of Records and the sky (X3D study)

An X3D 4.0 world (Immersive profile) drawn by X_ITE 16.6.0 (bundled in `vendor/x_ite/`, with its own LICENSE.md), with a page that opens translucent glass modals in three lenses (Technical, Wonder, Contested) and a claim ledger. v2 of the 13 Nov 2026 study; v1 (`../v1/`) is untouched and still works. Geometry is generated by `build/build_scene2.py` and is CC0. Claims keep their own authors.

**What is new since v1.** Real Blender Cycles light baked into vertex colour for three times of day. A real star sky (the star catalogue from the x3d-blender-io demo page plus 41 named stars) that you can scrub from 13,000 BC to 2100 AD, with Orion's Belt, Leo and the Orion Correlation overlay. A walkable imagined Hall of Records interior, labelled speculative. Three lens modals (32 Technical, 21 Wonder, 22 Contested cards) where every Contested card is told as claim, then evidence. Primary papers opened where free (Hawass 2007, Hassan 1944, Petrie 1883, the Big Void and North Face Corridor papers). Ground truth fixed against v1 (below).

**Three tiers, three colours.** Documented (cyan), theorized (amber), speculative (magenta). Layers are `Switch` nodes (`SW_DOC`, `SW_THE`, `SW_SPE`, plus `SW_ORION`, `SW_GAZE`, `SW_SKY`, `SW_CONST`). The ledger in the page has 25 rows (17 documented, 3 theorized, 5 speculative), each with its basis and a grade (Confirmed: source opened and read; Likely: consistent across sources or computed here from such inputs; Suspected: a summary or recollection).

**What is invented (Confirmed).** Every underground position in plan (the three Osiris shafts are offset 4 m and 6 m from each other to read clearly); the heights of Chamber I and the sizes of the six side chambers (drawn as equal boxes; the text gives each its own size); the compass orientation of every chamber; the Khufu interior beyond published lengths (positions and the Big Void's location are schematic); the Sphinx silhouette; the box that stands for the seismic anomaly (thickness 3 m); the whole interior of Cayce's Hall (box size, 16 columns, 26 niches, dais, orb, light) and its 26 m depth; the radius, spiral pitch and cube positions of the "648 m" claim; the one-system links and pulses; the causeway and valley temple shapes; the colour grading of the Blender bakes (exposure rules in states.json).

**Corrections to v1 (left as published).** The Great Pyramid entrance is 7.29 m east of the axis (v1 drew it west) and starts on the casing 13.3 m in from the edge; the King's Chamber's long axis is east-west (v1 had it north-south); the Queen's Chamber box was transposed; the Osiris Shaft now sits under Khafre's causeway, about halfway from the Sphinx to the funerary temple (Hawass 2007 p. 381), not 110 m west of the Sphinx; Chamber A has a published height; the claimed cubes under Khafre are 90 m a side in the coverage read (v1 drew 80 m); the "12 x 9 x 5 m" anomaly is about 9 by 12 m in plan and buried less than 5 m, so 5 m is a depth bound, not a height; the unsourced 41.48 m depth is flagged instead of drawn as fact; Hawass 2007 is CASAE 36 (not ASAE 36); the Orion Correlation is Bauval 1989 and 1994; the Great Pyramid, Khafre and Menkaure heights are labelled as today or original.

**Sky.** `stars_v2.json`: 978 stars within 50 light-years (HYG v4.1, from the x3d-blender-io demo page `docs/brag/stars.json`) rotated into the ICRS frame with one rotation fitted on Sirius and Procyon (eleven other stars land within 0.16 degrees); 41 named bright stars from CDS Sesame; Vondrak 2011 long-term precession matrices (ERFA `ltpb`) every 100 years; proper motion ignored (Orion's Belt unaffected; Arcturus about 8 degrees off at 10,500 BC).
