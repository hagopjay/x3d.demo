Gobbler, worlds within

The Gobbler demiurge page with four whole scenes nested inside the bird. Each hangs in a glass bubble round the Sun in the chest, inside the Dyson ribcage, at its own scale: Giza at about 1:3,000 of its own metres, the Pantheon at 1:58, the swatch shelf at 1:27, the Drillfield at 1:1,100. None of them is in the boot payload; each loads on navigation intent or immediate proximity, with a two-world residency budget and departure grace period, and the dive is the village's own (a geometric fall in distance, near and far planes that follow it, a logarithmic depth buffer).

Giza, disco night is Giza v2.1's plateau, Sphinx and Osiris Shaft with a party on top: a mirror ball over Khufu, beams, lasers from apex to apex, a dance floor on Khufu's summit and rings that pulse on the bird's heartbeat. Khufu turns to X-ray every 8 seconds. The Hall of Records under the Sphinx's left paw is hypothetical: everything drawn inside it is invented and should be read as fiction, not archaeological evidence.
