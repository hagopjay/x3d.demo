Binary X-Ray: the debugger lies

A small name-to-serial crackme, reverse-engineered by x3dray.binary. Six hypotheses, each re-run here as an experiment with a witness you can re-run. Under a debugger the program quietly poisons its own seed and hands you a serial that only works under the debugger. The city below is the binary: one building per function, baked in Blender, drawn in X3D 4.0.

The debugger lies

Both columns were run on one machine: gdb 15.1 and a plain argv run. The poisoned serial is not read out of memory; it is recomputed from the H5 model with the H2 salt and then judged by the binary.
