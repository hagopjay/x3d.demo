D3 to X3D Bridge v2.2 · v2.1 · v2 · v1

Sixteen ideas from the D3 / D3Blocks gallery, rebuilt as true 3D X3D scenes. D3 does the maths (hierarchy, force, chord, sankey, geo, scales, colour); a 300-line emitter, d3-x3d, writes the result as X3D 4.0, which an XSD validator checks and X_ITE draws. Every chart is fed by real data from these repositories: their commits, their X3D files, their folders and the X3D 4.0 type system itself. Each card says what the third dimension adds that the flat original cannot. New in v2: five flagships marked ★ (a living graph that grows through time, a clickable chord dome with flow beads, a sankey canyon with pulses and a fly-through, the project globe with great-circle arcs to the twin sites, a space-time cube sliced by a moving time slab) and screen-space label declutter on every scene.
