A library reading room painted as a Japanese sumi-e ink wash on warm rice paper: tall bookcases along both long walls, two rows of columns, long reading tables with benches and lamps, a large globe on a pedestal in the centre, three tall windows in the far wall, double doors at the other end. Black ink brushwork, soft grey washes, a few vermilion seal-red accents, visible paper grain, bare paper for the light.
