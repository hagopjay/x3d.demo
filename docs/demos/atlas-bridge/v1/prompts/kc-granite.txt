Deep rose-red speckled Aswan granite everywhere: the King's Chamber inside the Great Pyramid of Giza, a bare rectangular room whose walls, floor and ceiling are all dark pink-red granite with black and grey crystals, polished and slightly glossy, five wall courses of huge blocks with hairline joints, nine massive red granite beams across the flat ceiling, a worn red granite floor, one lidless chocolate-brown granite sarcophagus with a chipped corner near the west wall, a low square doorway in the corner of the north wall, lit by two warm work lamps on the floor, dust in the air, documentary photograph, sharp focus. No limestone, no sandstone, no yellow stone.
