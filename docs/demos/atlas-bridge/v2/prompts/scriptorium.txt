Photograph of the interior of a medieval Armenian monastery scriptorium. Walls of warm pink-orange tufa stone blocks, a pale lime-plastered barrel vault overhead, a dark polished basalt flagstone floor. Three small arched windows in one long wall let in soft daylight. A long oak table carries open vellum manuscripts and beeswax candles in iron dishes; oak benches stand on a worn red wool rug. Tall oak bookshelves are full of leather-bound books in oxblood, green, tan and blue. A wooden lectern holds an open book, an upright carved stone cross slab stands against the end wall, two stone columns flank an arched oak door, and there is an iron-banded oak chest and tall iron candle stands. Natural light, no people, sharp detail, 35 mm photograph.
