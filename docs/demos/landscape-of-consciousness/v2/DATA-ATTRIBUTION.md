## Takedown, and how to ask

If you own anything listed here (Robert Lawrence Kuhn, Alex Gomez-Marin, a named researcher, a publisher, a font owner) and want it removed or the credit changed,
open an issue at https://github.com/hagopjay/x3d.demo/issues titled `Takedown: landscape-of-consciousness` and say what you want removed.
It will be removed promptly after a request, without argument; the history can be cleaned on request.
