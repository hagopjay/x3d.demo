A Landscape of Consciousness, in X3D

Robert Lawrence Kuhn's taxonomy (figure by Alex Gomez-Marin) rebuilt as one navigable X3D 4.0 world: ten families as ten stations, 209 entries as beads you can fly to, and a river of thought to ride. Every label is geometry, so the text draws even where no font can be fetched.
