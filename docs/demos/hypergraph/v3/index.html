Sources and limits

ECO codes, names and move lines: Lichess chess-openings table, commit 3ba6b20, CC0, bundled under source/. Positions replayed with python-chess 1.10 to find transpositions. Layout: seeded 4D relaxation (tools/build.py); families are clusters, codes sit on a spiral inside. Drawn with Babylon.js 9.37 (WebGL, custom shaders); the portable scene is hypergraph.x3d (X3D 4.0, XSD-valid, static pose, view in X_ITE). The DSA set, engine concepts and 5 bridges are authored, not published. No game statistics: nothing here ranks openings. Full notes: NOTICE.md.
