Hypergraph, hyper-dimensionally

40 DSA concepts are points in four dimensions (x, y, z and w = difficulty tier). 15 hyperedges each join 4 to 7 concepts at once; each is a hub with spokes and a translucent bound. Spin the 4D rotation planes to trade difficulty for space, drop to 2D or 3D, or flip to the pairwise "clique expansion" to see what an ordinary graph does to a hyperedge.
