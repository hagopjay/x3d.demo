# Hypergraph v2: DSA concepts plus Chess ECO A to E

**What it is.** The v1 hypergraph (40 DSA concepts, 15 hyperedges; v1 is untouched) with the Chess ECO classification overlaid in the same 4D space. 48 openings are boxes, w = ECO volume A to E; 8 chess-engine concepts are cones, w = tier; DSA concepts stay spheres, w = tier. 36 hyperedges: 15 DSA, 16 chess (the five ECO volumes plus Sicilian family, 1.e4 e5 open games, Gambits, Hypermodern, Fianchetto structures, Queen-pawn closed, Solid defences, Sharp counterplay, Indian defences, and two engine groups) and 5 bridges that mix both domains (search is recursion; transposition = memoization by hashing; opening book is a trie; move ordering is a priority sort; iterative deepening = BFS by DFS). A layer switch shows DSA, Chess, or both with the bridges.

**Sources and grades.**
- ECO names and code ranges: the standard ECO classification. The major families (Reti, English, Bird, Dutch, Benko, Budapest, Pirc, Modern, Alekhine, Scandinavian, Caro-Kann, Sicilian Dragon/Scheveningen/Najdorf, French, Vienna, King's Gambit, Philidor, Petrov, Scotch, Four Knights, Italian, Evans, Two Knights, Ruy Lopez, QGD, QGA, Slav, Semi-Slav, Grunfeld, Catalan, Queen's and Bogo-Indian, Nimzo-Indian) were cross-checked on 18 Nov 2026 against the Wikipedia "List of chess openings" table (the fetch covered about the first 100,000 of 167,000 characters, so E89-E99 were not read). Minor ranges (Danish, Latvian, Smith-Morra, Sveshnikov, Kan, Taimanov, Veresov, London, Colle, Trompowsky, Tarrasch) are from memory: **Likely, not Confirmed**.
- Groupings and the five bridges are the author's judgement, not a published taxonomy. `x3d.chess` has no ECO data (checked: its studies.json and viewer carry none), so this overlay is standalone; it does not read x3d.chess.
- Layout: seeded 4D relaxation (`tools/build.py`, seed 20261118).

**Checks (Confirmed, 18 Nov 2026).** `hypergraph.x3d` is valid against the X3D 4.0 XSD via x3d_mcp `validate_xml` (lxml 6.1.3, receipt-xsd.json). Headless Chromium (Playwright, SwiftShader software GL), X_ITE 16.6.0, 1300x900: 96 of 96 nodes and 36 of 36 hyperedge switches resolved; 36 hyperedges visible with both layers, 15 with DSA, 16 with Chess; selecting `eng:transposition-table` reports 6 hyper-neighbours (matches data); 0 console errors (receipt-browser.json, tools/smoke.js). 3.9 frames/s is software GL on a CPU container with 96 nodes and labels, not a GPU figure.

**Computed counts (data.json).** All layers: 96 concepts, 36 hyperedges, 233 incidences, 550 distinct pairs after clique expansion (2.36 per incidence). Chess alone: 56 nodes, 16 hyperedges, 132 incidences, 360 pairs (2.73 per incidence). Bridges: 5 hyperedges, 28 incidences.
