What this does not say

  • Registered, drawn or bridged is not the same as correct. The three.js picture numbers are against X_ITE, which is the reference, not the truth; a node X_ITE draws wrongly would match here.
  • The Babylon, Castle and Blender columns were not re-measured. Babylon is the tracker's word, Castle is a scene count from one scout run, Blender is a word match in source (Suspected).
  • Software GL only. No real GPU, no frame rates.
  • Most fixtures cover a node's main path, not every field. The gaps are in each fixture's receipt in x3d.loom.
Generated by a script from the x3d.loom trackers and the fixtures re-run on this build: tools/gen.py, tools/probe.js, tools/build.py in this folder, inputs and counts in receipts-summary.json. Nothing here is third-party imagery.
