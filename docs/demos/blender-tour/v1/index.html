A tour of Blender, through X3D eyes

Blender 5.3 driven by script in a real window: Geometry and Simulation Nodes, rigid bodies and cloth, four renderers on one scene, keyframes, drivers and Grease Pencil, then eight procedural demos cut into a 45-second reel in Blender's own Video Sequencer. At every stop the same question: what survives an X3D export, and what does X_ITE show?

45.0 s, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. Every frame procedural (EEVEE in Blender 5.3); the soundtrack is synthesized by build/reel.py (no third-party audio). The X3D segment is an X3D world played by the add-on's own behaviour-graph Player, with a scripted click on Terra.

Stop 1 · Geometry Nodes and a Simulation Zone

A grid scattered with crystals whose size breathes with 4D noise driven by Scene Time (no keyframes), and 600 fireflies whose positions persist frame to frame in a Simulation Zone.

Stop 2 · Physics, and RigidBody import

A 30-block tower written as an X3D RigidBodyCollection imports at the file's heights (0.125 to 2.375 m; issue #99's fix), then Blender's Bullet solver and a kinematic wrecking ball (handed to the solver at frame 45) take it down while silk cloth drapes a pedestal.

Build

build/tour.py (stops 1 to 4 in a live window with a caption overlay), build/wild.py (the eight demos; --clip renders 5 s), build/x3d_clip.py (an X3D behaviour world played by the Player), build/reel.py (the Video Sequencer edit, live or in the background), build/xite_shot.cjs (X_ITE screenshots). Set X3D_IO_SOURCE / X3D_IO_REPO to a checkout of x3d-blender-io. See README.md for what was and was not checked.
