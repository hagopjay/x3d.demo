X3D in the AI era · the glb tooling lane, checked

The optimiser that has to keep the names

A glb optimiser can cut a file by a factor of ten and still be worthless to X3D: if it merges nodes, the DEF names that the Blender add-on wrote into the glb extras are gone, and nothing can say which node was which. X3D is the 3D format an AI can be held accountable to only while those names survive. This page runs the x3d.gltf lane's own optimiser and gates in your browser on four real scenes, then checks the output against the source with code that is not the lane's.

Limits, stated

    Sources. The optimiser and gates are the x3d.gltf lane of x3d.loom (glTF-Transform, meshoptimizer, Draco, KTX2, Khronos glTF-Validator), bundled for the browser as forge.bundle.js (409,854 bytes, SHA-256 …). Receipts: forge-receipts.json. Scene files: PRISMATICA Aurora Cathedral, Asterion and Still life are read from the bridge and blenderizer page's scenes folder (SHA-256 checked against the receipts in section 5's table); LUMEN and the precomputed Draco and ETC1S files are in this page's scenes folder. three.js loads from jsDelivr at a pinned version.

    Versions kept. This is glb Forge, version 1. Nothing earlier is overwritten. Independent work: not endorsed by the Khronos Group, the Web3D Consortium, or the maintainers of glTF-Transform, meshoptimizer, Draco or three.js.
