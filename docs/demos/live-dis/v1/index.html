What this is

    
DIS (Distributed Interactive Simulation, IEEE 1278.1) is how separate simulators share one world. Each simulator sends small binary messages, called PDUs, over UDP: "entity 1:1:1 is here, heading this way, at this speed". Nobody sends a full state at 60 frames a second. A receiver draws each entity by dead reckoning: it extrapolates from the last PDU with one of nine standard formulas until the next PDU arrives. The sender runs the same formula and sends a new PDU only when the receiver's guess would be too far off.

    
X3D 4.0 has six nodes for DIS. X_ITE 16.8.1 reads all six and all of their fields, but does nothing with them. A small runtime on this page gives them their behaviour: it decodes PDU octets and writes the values into the fields of the X_ITE nodes, and it encodes PDUs from the fields of the nodes that are set to write.

    

      
EspduTransform
A Transform that follows one entity. Its translation and rotation come from Entity State PDUs and are dead-reckoned between them. Here: the aircraft and the rover read, the buoy writes.

      
DISEntityManager
Watches a network channel and reports entities nobody declared. Here it finds the ship and the helicopter, and reports the helicopter gone when it has been silent for 12 seconds. Two ROUTEs in the X3D file add and remove them from the scene.

      
DISEntityTypeMapping
Says which model file to show for which entity type. Here: any surface platform gets the ship, an air platform of category 21 gets the helicopter, anything else a magenta ball.

      
TransmitterPdu
The state of one radio: on, transmitting, frequency, power, antenna position. Here a ROUTE from its transmitState turns on the red sphere around the aircraft.

      
SignalPdu
What a radio is sending: here, audio samples. The trace above is drawn from its data field.

      
ReceiverPdu
What one radio is hearing and how strongly. Here a ROUTE from its receiverState lights the green lamp on the rover.

    

    
What is real and what is not. The PDU octets are real DIS 7 PDUs: the codec was checked field by field against two other open implementations. The exercise is invented: four entities and one radio, moving along formulas near an arbitrary point of the Pacific Ocean, on a flat local plane. No PDU here was captured from any network or simulator. The network path (UDP, a gateway, a WebSocket) was run on one machine only; this page has not been tried against another vendor's simulator.
